# SourceLight's name and logo

The SourceLight source code is open source, under the
[Mozilla Public License 2.0](LICENSE). The name **SourceLight** and the
SourceLight logo and app icon are not. They are trademarks of James Tully,
and the MPL-2.0 grants no rights to them (section 2.3).

The name says that a build comes from the SourceLight project. This policy
keeps that true, while leaving the code free to use.

## You may, without asking

- Say what your work is: "a fork of SourceLight", "based on SourceLight",
  "a plugin for SourceLight".
- Write about SourceLight, review it, teach it and link to it, by name, with
  the logo when you are showing the official app.
- Share unmodified official builds, under their own name and icon.

## You may not, without written permission

- Distribute a modified build, a fork, or anything built from changed source
  under the name SourceLight or with its logo or icon. Give it a different
  name and icon that can't be confused with SourceLight's.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests the SourceLight project made,
  endorses or supports your product, service or build.
- Use SourceLight, or a confusingly similar name, as the name of your own
  product, company, domain or app-store listing.

The app's identifier (`app.sourcelight`) and its XMP namespace
(`http://ns.sourcelight.app/xmp/1.0/`) belong to the official app as well. A
fork should use its own, so the two can be installed side by side and their
sidecar records stay apart.

Not sure whether a use is fine? Open an issue and ask.
