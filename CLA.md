Version 1.0

Thank you for contributing to SourceLight. This agreement is between you and
the maintainer of SourceLight. It gives the maintainer the rights needed to
look after the project: to publish your contribution, to offer SourceLight
under other licences as well as the open-source one, and to hand the project
on. You keep the copyright in what you contribute.

You sign it once, by commenting on your first pull request (below). It then
covers everything you contribute.

- **"Contribution"** means any work you submit to the SourceLight project

  you clearly mark as "Not a Contribution" when you send it is not one.

## 8. Signing

The first time you open a pull request, a bot asks you to sign. Reply on the
pull request with:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

That records your GitHub account, the date and this version of the agreement.
It covers that pull request and every Contribution you make afterwards, until
you tell the Maintainer in writing that it no longer applies to new ones.
